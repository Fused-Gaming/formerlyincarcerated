# Board Governance Structure - Phase 0

## 13-Member Board Composition

### Formerly Incarcerated Representatives (4)
- **Board Chair** | Criminal justice reform advocate, lived experience
- **Member** | Reentry services leader, housing advocate
- **Member** | Technology/blockchain community member, lived experience
- **Member** | Education/mentorship leader, lived experience

### Developer/Technology Leaders (3)
- **CTO Liaison** | Chief Technology Officer (ex-officio)
- **Blockchain Engineer** | Bitcoin/smart contract expert
- **Impact Tech Lead** | Data systems, outcomes measurement specialist

### Finance & Legal (3)
- **Treasurer** | CFO or financial management expert
- **General Counsel** | Criminal justice attorney
- **Compliance Officer** | Regulatory/nonprofit governance expert

### Government & Policy (3)
- **Oakland City Representative** | Housing/reentry services director
- **San Francisco City Representative** | Policy/partnership liaison
- **State Policy Advocate** | Criminal justice reform, asset recovery specialist

## Committee Structure

### Finance Committee (5 members)
- Treasurer (Chair), CFO, General Counsel, 2 at-large
- Monthly review of budget, investments, grant applications
- Quarterly financial statements, audit oversight

### Governance Committee (5 members)
- Board Chair (Chair), General Counsel, Compliance Officer, 2 at-large
- Board recruitment, orientation, evaluation
- Policy updates, conflict of interest management

### Outcomes Committee (5 members)
- Impact Tech Lead (Chair), Program Manager, Community Liaison, 2 at-large
- Participant metrics, impact measurement framework
- Quarterly impact reports, evaluation methodology

### Partnerships Committee (4 members)
- Board Chair (Co-Chair), CTO Liaison (Co-Chair), 2 at-large
- Government relations, developer partnerships
- Funding strategy, ecosystem alignment

## Meeting Cadence

- **Quarterly Public Board Meetings** | 60-90 minutes | Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 | Publicly livestreamed
- **Monthly Executive Committee** | 90 minutes | Finance, Governance, Compliance review
- **Weekly Operations Standup** | 30 minutes | ED, COO, CTO, key directors | Informal, async summary available
- **Committee Meetings** | 60 minutes | Monthly or as-needed per committee

## Decision-Making Framework

### Simple Majority (>50% votes required)
- Regular operational approvals
- Committee recommendations
- Staff hiring/termination decisions
- Monthly budget adjustments (<10% variance)

### Supermajority 2/3 (≥67% votes required)
- Major capital allocation (>$100K)
- Strategic pivots or program changes
- Participant incentive structure modifications
- Asset allocation or treasury decisions
- Amendment of governance documents
- Removal of board members

### Unanimous Consent (expedited, <5 days)
- Emergency operational decisions
- Urgent partnership agreements
- Time-sensitive compliance matters
- Unanimous board waiver of Roberts Rules

### Stakeholder Input Required
- Major funding source changes
- Participant allocation policy changes
- Community partner terminations
- Annual strategic priorities

## Board Member Expectations

- **Time commitment:** 20-25 hours/month (meetings + committees + prep)
- **Financial pledge:** Minimum $5K-$25K annual (tiered by capacity)
- **Expertise sharing:** Pro-bono advisory hours for major initiatives
- **Attendance:** 75% minimum meeting attendance
- **Confidentiality:** Full NDA on sensitive information
