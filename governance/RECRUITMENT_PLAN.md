# Recruitment Plan - Phase 0 Hiring Strategy

## Critical Path: Executive Director + CTO (Weeks 1-3)

### Executive Director Search
**Timeline:** Weeks 1-3 (concurrent with CTO search)
**Process:** External executive search firm + board network

**Criteria:**
- 10+ years nonprofit/social enterprise leadership
- Track record: fundraising ($10M+), team building (50+), impact measurement
- Criminal justice reform passion (lived experience or deep advocacy)
- Comfort with crypto/blockchain technologies
- Ability to work with limited resources in early stage

**Search channels:**
- Executive search firm (28-day accelerated process)
- Board member network (prior executives at social enterprises)
- Idealist, LinkedIn, Taproot Foundation
- Peer referrals from similar orgs (Code2040, Homeboy Industries, etc.)

**Deliverables by Week 3:**
- 3-5 qualified candidates screened
- 2-3 semifinalists conducting preliminary interviews
- Timeline: Offer by Week 6, start by Week 8

**Compensation:** $180K-$220K + equity (0.5-1%)

### Chief Technology Officer Search
**Timeline:** Weeks 1-3 (concurrent with ED search)
**Process:** Crypto/fintech professional networks + technical interview

**Criteria:**
- Bitcoin/blockchain infrastructure experience (Ethereum, Lightning, L2s)
- Able to architect system for asset recovery and deed-restriction
- Team leadership experience (managed 3+ engineers)
- Startup mentality (limited resources, rapid iteration)
- Prior nonprofit or impact tech background preferred

**Search channels:**
- Crypto foundation networks (Coin Center, Gnosis, Open Zeppelin)
- LinkedIn/Twitter crypto engineering community
- Prior employer networks (Coinbase, Consensys, Protocol Labs, etc.)
- YCombinator, Techstars alumni networks
- Direct outreach to known impact tech builders

**Deliverables by Week 3:**
- 5-8 qualified candidates screened
- 2-3 semifinalists completing technical interviews
- Offer by Week 5, start by Week 7

**Compensation:** $160K-$200K + equity (0.75-2%)

## Wave 2: Executive Team (Weeks 2-4)

### Chief Operating Officer
**Timeline:** Weeks 2-4 | Reports to ED
- Nonprofit operations/compliance background
- Prior roles: Operations Director, Program Director, Exec Coach
- Channels: Board referrals, idealist.org, nonprofit consultant networks
- Comp: $140K-$160K

### Chief Financial Officer / Finance Director
**Timeline:** Weeks 2-4 | Reports to ED
- Nonprofit or social enterprise finance experience
- Prior roles: Finance Director, Grant Manager, Controller
- Channels: Board referral, Foundation Center, nonprofit boards
- Comp: $130K-$155K (or shared CFO role with ED initially)

### Community Programs Director
**Timeline:** Weeks 2-4 | Reports to ED
- Reentry services or housing program leadership
- Prior roles: Program Manager, Director of Reentry, Community Liaison
- Channels: Reentry professional networks, Bay Area nonprofits
- Comp: $110K-$140K

## Wave 3: Technical & Community (Weeks 3-6)

### Lead Engineer (Smart Contracts/Protocol)
**Timeline:** Weeks 3-5
- 5+ years Solidity or smart contract development
- Prior roles: Senior Engineer at crypto/Web3 company
- Background checks required (crypto security protocols)
- Comp: $130K-$160K + equity (0.25-0.5%)

### Protocol Engineer (Bitcoin/Lightning)
**Timeline:** Weeks 3-5
- Bitcoin/Lightning protocol expertise
- Prior roles: Protocol researcher, Bitcoin Core contributor, Layer 2 engineer
- Security-focused background essential
- Comp: $120K-$150K + equity (0.25-0.5%)

### Junior/Full-Stack Engineer
**Timeline:** Weeks 4-6
- 2-4 years full-stack (JavaScript/TypeScript)
- React, Node.js, PostgreSQL experience
- First impact-focused role or nonprofit tech experience welcomed
- Comp: $90K-$120K

### Three Community Liaisons (Weeks 4-6)
- **Reentry Services Liaison:** 3+ years reentry/social services
  - Comp: $65K-$85K
- **Housing/Developer Liaison:** Real estate or property management background
  - Comp: $65K-$85K
- **Program/Outcomes Liaison:** Data or program management background
  - Comp: $70K-$90K

### Data Analyst
**Timeline:** Weeks 4-6
- SQL, Python, data visualization experience
- Nonprofit or social impact analytics preferred
- Comp: $85K-$110K

## Recruitment Timeline Summary

```
Week 1-3:  ED + CTO search (external search firm + networks)
Week 2-4:  COO, CFO, Community Dir interviews + offers
Week 3-5:  2 Senior Engineers (smart contracts, protocol)
Week 4-6:  3 Community Liaisons + Analytics roles
Week 5+:   Junior Engineer, onboarding ramp-up
```

**Total hiring duration:** 6-8 weeks from Week 1 start date
**Team fully onboarded:** Week 12 (staggered start dates by role priority)

---

## Diversity & Inclusion Commitments

- **Priority:** At least 50% of hires from historically marginalized communities
- **Formerly incarcerated hires:** Target 3-4 roles (community liaisons, program management)
- **Women in tech leadership:** Target 40%+ of technical team
- **Equitable compensation:** Compressed salary bands (3:1 range vs. 10:1 standard tech)
- **Paid interview process:** $250 interview stipend for community candidates
