# Advisory Board Structure - Phase 0

## 5-Member Advisory Board (Non-voting)

Advisory board members provide specialized expertise without voting rights or fiduciary duty. They serve 2-year terms with quarterly meetings.

### 1. Dignifi Partnership Representative
**Role:** Reentry Services & Program Integration
- **Liaison to:** Dignifi's rehabilitation and employment programs
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Advise on participant enrollment and support pathways
  - Coordinate cross-promotion and integrated services
  - Review program outcomes and participant satisfaction
  - Ensure alignment with Dignifi's mission
- **Meeting cadence:** Quarterly advisory + monthly program sync
- **Compensation:** Waived (organizational partnership benefit)

### 2. Oakland City Government Liaison
**Role:** Local Policy & Housing Integration
- **Title:** Director of Housing/Reentry Services (Oakland)
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Navigate city permitting and zoning for deed-restricted properties
  - Facilitate partnerships with Oakland housing programs
  - Advise on city incentive structures and tax implications
  - Support workforce development coordination
- **Meeting cadence:** Quarterly advisory + bi-monthly city coordination
- **Compensation:** None (government position)

### 3. San Francisco City Government Liaison
**Role:** Regional Policy & Expansion Strategy
- **Title:** Policy Director or Chief Economist (SF)
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Advise on SF expansion strategy and regulatory framework
  - Connect to SF reentry and housing initiatives
  - Provide insights on asset recovery and property mechanisms
  - Support legislative advocacy
- **Meeting cadence:** Quarterly advisory + as-needed policy consultation
- **Compensation:** None (government position)

### 4. Housing Developer Representative
**Role:** Real Estate & Property Operations
- **Background:** Experienced developer with affordable housing focus
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Review acquisition strategy and property valuation
  - Advise on construction budgets and project timelines
  - Connect to developer networks and vendor relationships
  - Ensure operational efficiency and property management
- **Meeting cadence:** Quarterly advisory + project-specific reviews
- **Compensation:** $2K-$5K per quarter (consultant fee) or equity consideration

### 5. Academic Evaluation Partner
**Role:** Research & Impact Measurement
- **Affiliation:** UC Berkeley, Stanford, or comparable institution
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Design rigorous evaluation methodology
  - Advise on data collection and analysis
  - Ensure IRB compliance and research ethics
  - Publish peer-reviewed outcomes research
  - Connect to academic funding opportunities
- **Meeting cadence:** Quarterly advisory + semi-monthly research planning
- **Compensation:** Research grants and publication rights

## Advisory Board Governance

**Charter:**
- Non-voting status on strategic decisions
- Direct reporting to Board Chair and ED
- Quarterly meeting summaries distributed to full board
- Annual renewal contingent on engagement and value

**Engagement Timeline:**
- Q1 2026: Dignifi and Oakland liaisons confirmed
- Q2 2026: Housing developer and SF liaison recruited
- Q3 2026: Academic partner and full board onboarded
- Q4 2026: First comprehensive advisory review

**Escalation Path:**
Advisory recommendations that impact:
- >$50K decisions → Finance Committee review
- Program design changes → Outcomes Committee review
- Government/partnership changes → Partnerships Committee review

## Integration with Governance Structure

```
Advisory Board (5)
        |
    Reports to: Board Chair + ED
        |
    ├─ Dignifi Rep → Programs Committee
    ├─ Oakland Gov → Partnerships Committee
    ├─ SF Gov → Partnerships Committee
    ├─ Developer → Finance/Operations Committee
    └─ Academic → Outcomes Committee
```
