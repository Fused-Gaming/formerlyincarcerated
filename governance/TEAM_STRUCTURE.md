## Department Breakdown

### Leadership (3)
- **Executive Director** | $180-220K | Reports to Board | Vision, fundraising, partnerships
- **Chief Operating Officer** | $140-160K | Reports to ED | Operations, compliance, day-to-day
- **Chief Technology Officer** | $160-200K | Reports to ED | Protocol, infrastructure, team

### Operations (4)
- **Operations Manager** | $90-110K | Reports to COO | Workflow, systems, vendor management
- **Finance & Accounting Specialist** | $85-105K | Reports to CFO | Bookkeeping, reporting, grants
- **Community Manager** | $75-95K | Reports to COO | Stakeholder relations, events
- **HR & Admin Specialist** | $70-90K | Reports to COO | Recruiting, benefits, compliance

### Technology (3)
- **Lead Engineer** | $130-160K | Reports to CTO | Smart contracts, systems design
- **Protocol Engineer** | $120-150K | Reports to CTO | Bitcoin integration, validation
- **Junior/Full-Stack Engineer** | $90-120K | Reports to Lead | Frontend, API, DevOps

### Community (3)
- **Community Liaison (Reentry Services)** | $65-85K | Reports to Comm Mgr | Participant enrollment, support
- **Community Liaison (Housing)** | $65-85K | Reports to Comm Mgr | Property partnerships, landlord relations
- **Program Manager** | $80-100K | Reports to Comm Mgr | Impact measurement, participant retention

### Analytics (1-2)
- **Data Analyst** | $85-110K | Reports to CFO | Metrics, reporting, dashboards
- **Outcomes Officer** (part-time) | $40-60K | Reports to Program Mgr | Evaluation, compliance tracking

## Compensation Strategy

- **Total annual payroll (15 FTE):** ~$1.7M
- **Range spread:** 3:1 (entry $70K → leadership $220K)
- **Benefits:** Health, 401(k), 5 weeks PTO, professional development
- **Equity consideration:** 0.25-2% options for technical leadership
