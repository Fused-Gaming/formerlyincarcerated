# Brand & Communications Strategy - Phase 0-1

## Overview
Establish recognizable brand identity, launch social media presence, build media relationships

---

## Brand Identity

### Logo & Visual System
**Status:** Use existing HP brand (per CLAUDE.md)
- Primary color: `hp-orange` (#F7931A)
- Secondary: `hp-black` (#050505), `hp-cream` (#F6F1E8)
- Font: Bebas Neue (display), Inter (body)

**Logo Variants:**
1. Full wordmark + icon (horizontal, 1200px)
2. Icon only (square, 512px)
3. Stacked version (vertical, 800px)
4. Favicon (32px, transparent bg)

**Usage guidelines:**
- Minimum size: 100px width
- Clear space: 20px on all sides
- Never rotate, distort, or change colors
- Dark backgrounds: Use cream logo
- Light backgrounds: Use black logo

### Messaging Framework

**Core positioning:**
Problem → Solution → Impact

**Problem statement:**
"500K+ incarcerated people release annually. 80% experience housing insecurity. Failed reentry = wasted human potential + $34B taxpayer cost."

**Solution statement:**
"Bitcoin Land Bond combines deed-restricted housing (permanently affordable) with blockchain transparency to create sustainable, scalable solutions for formerly incarcerated individuals."

**Impact statement:**
"Stable housing + community support = lower recidivism, higher employment, stronger communities."

**Brand voice:** Hopeful, direct, data-driven, resident-centered

### Interview Talking Points

1. **"Why criminal justice housing now?"**
   "Mass incarceration created 2M+ people with criminal records. 80% face 
   housing discrimination. Deed-restricted housing removes that barrier."

2. **"How does deed-restricted housing work?"**
   "Residents buy at deep discount (50-70% market value). Housing stays 
   affordable forever through deed restriction. No speculation. Community 
   stays."

3. **"What makes this different?"**
   "Most nonprofits fight short-term fires. We're building permanent, 
   scalable infrastructure. Blockchain transparency shows every dollar impact."

4. **"Recidivism outcomes?"**
   "Stable housing + community support = 50%+ reduction in reincarceration 
   vs. national average."
