## Middle of Funnel (Weeks 4-6): Engagement & Applications

### Weekly Info Sessions
**Oakland Sessions:** Thursdays 6pm, The Ella Baker Center
**SF Sessions:** Tuesdays 6pm, Code Tenderloin

**Session format (30 min):**
- 5 min: What is Bitcoin Land Bond?
- 5 min: How housing works + resident benefits
- 10 min: Q&A
- 5 min: Application walkthrough
- 5 min: 1-on-1 questions

**Attendance goal:** 25+ per session (500+ total by week 10)

### Website Application (Week 4)
**URL:** /apply
**Fields:**
- Name, contact info
- Reentry timeline (currently incarcerated/released)
- Housing needs assessment
- Employment status
- Community references
- Screening questions (non-disqualifying)

**Auto-response:** "Thanks for applying! We'll contact you within 48 hours."

## Bottom of Funnel (Weeks 7-10): Enrollment & Matching

### Fast-Track Enrollment (1-2 weeks)
1. **Week 1:** Initial screening call + housing preference survey
2. **Week 2:** Reference checks + background confirmation
3. **Week 3:** Lease signing + move-in coordination

### Buddy System Matching
- Pair new residents with 6+ month residents
- 4-week buddy orientation program
- Monthly buddy meetups

**Metrics:**
- Application completion rate: 80%+
- Enrollment time: 10-14 days avg
- Buddy satisfaction: 90%+

### FAQ Document
Q: Will I be evicted if I'm re-incarcerated?
A: No. Deed-restricted means your housing is protected. We support re-entry, not eviction.

Q: How much does it cost?
A: 30% of your income, capped at $800/month. No hidden fees.

Q: What if I'm still in prison?
A: Great! We can pre-apply you now, begin housing search 90 days before release.
