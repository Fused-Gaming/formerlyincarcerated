## HUD Second Chance Housing Program

### Program Details
**Second Chance Housing Competitive Grant:**
- Federal grant: $250K-$1M per award
- Focus: Housing for individuals with criminal records
- Deadline: Spring 2026
- Match requirement: 25% ($62.5K-$250K)

### Eligibility Requirements
- 501(c)(3) nonprofit OR partnership with nonprofit
- Serve people within 2 years of release
- Demonstrate community support + partnerships

### Approach
**Week 2:** Establish 501(c)(3) partnership (if needed)
- Partner with established nonprofit (Delancey Street, etc.)
- Outline collaboration agreement

**Week 4-6:** Gather community letters of support
- Oakland/SF government endorsements
- Resident testimonials
- Reentry organization partnerships

**Week 8-10:** Prepare HUD application
- Detailed resident selection criteria
- Housing maintenance plan
- 3-year budget + sustainability model

**Week 10-12:** Submit HUD grant + begin quarterly reporting prep

**Reentry Program Grants:**
- $100K-$500K awards

- Deadline: September 2026
- Lead time: 6-9 months

### Workforce Innovation & Opportunity Act (WIOA)

**WIOA Reentry Grants:**
- $300K-$1M for employment training + placement

- Our angle: Pair housing (Bitcoin Land Bond) with job training

**Contact:** Bay Area Workforce Board
