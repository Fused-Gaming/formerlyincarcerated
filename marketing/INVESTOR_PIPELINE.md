### Seed Round Structure
**Raise:** $2.5M

- Land acquisition: $1.2M (40%)
- Construction/renovation: $1M (33%)
- Operations + team: $300K (10%)
- Marketing + resident support: $200K (7%)
- Reserve/contingency: $300K (10%)

### Unit Economics
**Per-resident cost:** $60K build-out, $12K annual operations
**Revenue model:** 
- Housing: 30% of income (avg. $240/month/resident)
- Government contracts: $500/month/resident (employment services)
- Impact fund returns: 2-5% annual blended

**Payback timeline:** 7-9 years
**10-year resident impact:** 500 residents, $3M+ community revenue
