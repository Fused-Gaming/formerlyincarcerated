# Bitcoin Land Bond - Property Acquisition Workflow

## Real Estate Partnership & MLS Analysis (Week 6)

**Objective:** Identify qualified brokers, establish data feeds, refine acquisition criteria

**Activities:**
1. **Broker Recruitment (Days 1-3)**
   - Target: 2 brokers per city (experienced in deed restrictions, non-profit work)
   - Oakland: Bridges Housing, Community Housing Partnership broker networks
   - SF: Habitat for Humanity Bay Area, BRIDGE Housing brokers
   - Compensation: 0.5% finder fee (below standard 1-2.5%) paid at close

2. **MLS Integration (Days 4-5)**
   - Set up automated feeds: 50-100 unit range, distressed/bank-owned preferred
   - Price bands: Oakland $350-550K/unit, SF $600-900K/unit
   - Neighborhoods: West/East Oakland, Mission/Tenderloin SF
   - Flag: Tax-delinquent, foreclosure, owner-motivated sales

3. **Criteria Refinement (Days 6-7)**
   - 5+ unit minimum per property (efficiency)
   - Rehab budget: $50-100K per unit (SF higher end)
   - ADA accessibility must be achievable (not prohibitive cost)
   - Community board support verification

---

## Property Identification & Screening (Week 7)

**Objective:** Source 5-10 candidates per city, validate preliminary financials

**Process:**
1. **Initial Triage (Days 1-3)**
   - Broker presents 15-20 candidates per city
   - Internal scoring: location (30%), condition (25%), price (25%), community fit (20%)
   - Filter to 8-10 per city (top 50%)

2. **Preliminary Due Diligence (Days 4-5)**
   - Title search & lien check (1-2 days)
   - Property records review (zoning, deed history, violations)
   - Neighborhood demographic snapshot
   - Environmental screening (Phase 1 preliminary)

3. **Broker Negotiation (Days 6-7)**
   - Negotiate asking price (-10-15% target, market-dependent)
   - Establish inspection contingency (7-10 days)
   - Request seller disclosure, HOA docs, utility records
   - Shortlist to 5 per city for formal underwriting

---

## Underwriting & Inspections (Week 8)

**Objective:** Validate financials, identify rehab needs, secure lender pre-approval

**Deliverables:**
1. **Financial Underwriting (Days 1-3)**
   - Cap rate analysis (target 5-6%)
   - 30-year cash flow projection
   - Debt service coverage ratio (target 1.25x+)
   - Compare funding scenarios: Federal + Bitcoin donation mix

2. **Physical Inspections (Days 2-5)**
   - Full property inspection + inspector report
   - Structural engineer assessment
   - ADA accessibility survey
   - Rehab scope & budget validation
   - Environmental Phase 1 (full)

3. **Appraisal & Lender Review (Days 4-7)**
   - Order appraisals per lender requirement
   - Underwriter review & approval conditions
   - Insurance quote & confirmation
   - Final lender pre-approval (2-3 properties per city)

---

## Financing & Closing (Weeks 9-10)

**Objective:** Secure funding, execute purchase, record deed restrictions

**Week 9 - Financing:**
1. **Funding Assembly (Days 1-3)**
   - Bitcoin donation liquidation (weekly tranches)
   - Federal grant draw-down requests
   - Lender loan commitment letters
   - Ensure 20%+ down payment (reduce monthly mortgage)

2. **Purchase Agreement Negotiation (Days 3-5)**
   - Submit offers on 2 selected properties/city
   - Negotiate closing date (30-45 days target)
   - Include deed restriction requirement (non-negotiable)
   - Inspection contingency, appraisal gap clause

3. **Pre-Closing (Days 5-7)**
   - Schedule title company closing
   - Prepare closing disclosure & HUD-1 forms
   - Coordinate with lender final approval
   - Obtain proof of insurance

**Week 10 - Closing & Recording:**
1. **Closing Meeting (Days 1-2)**
   - Sign documents (deed, promissory note, security agreement)
   - Wire down payment + closing costs
   - Receive keys, coordinate property access

2. **Deed Restriction Recording (Days 2-3)**
   - Record deed of trust with county recorder
   - Deed restriction on title (permanent affordability, 99 years)
   - File evidence of restriction with IRS (Form 8609-A equivalent)

3. **Blockchain Registration (Days 3-5)**
   - Mint property deed as NFT (via Vercel/Supabase + blockchain partner)
   - Smart contract encoding affordability terms
   - Create immutable proof of restriction (Ethereum/Polygon)
   - Update CLAUDE.md with blockchain addresses

---

## Rehabilitation & Unit Preparation (Week 11)

**Objective:** Upgrade units for immediate occupancy, establish move-in ready timeline

**Activities:**
1. **Rehab Project Management (Days 1-3)**
   - Secure General Contractor (competitive bid, local hire preference)
   - Finalize scope from Week 8 inspection report
   - Establish 21-day completion timeline
   - Weekly progress check-ins

2. **Unit Improvements:**
   - Paint, flooring, fixtures (cosmetic)
   - Kitchen & bathroom upgrades
   - ADA modifications (bathrooms, entryways)
   - Appliances, window treatments, furniture

3. **Compliance & Move-In (Days 15-21)**
   - Final inspection & building permits sign-off
   - Utility setup (electric, gas, water, internet)
   - Pest control, cleaning
   - Resident move-in coordination (Week 12)
