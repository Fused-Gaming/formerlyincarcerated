# Bitcoin Land Bond - Pilot City Strategy

## Phase 1-2 Dual-City Approach (Oakland & San Francisco)

### Oakland Operations

**Geographic Focus:**
- West Oakland: Deep historical ties to reentry infrastructure, strong community orgs
- East Oakland: Underserved population, existing housing crisis, emerging partnerships
- Target: 25 residents per neighborhood (50 total)

**Community Partnerships:**
- Reentry organizations (Delancey Street, St. Vincent de Paul, Rebuild)
- Supervisors District Offices (West/East Oakland reps)
- Local nonprofits (East Bay Community Law Center, CCRC)
- Faith leaders (church networks for trust-building)

**Risk Management:**
- NIMBY mitigation through community advisory committee
- Monthly town halls in both neighborhoods
- Transparent communication on program outcomes

---

### San Francisco Operations

**Geographic Focus:**
- DPH Reentry Initiative alignment (Mission/Tenderloin)
- Tech industry anchoring (downtown partnerships for employment)
- Anchor tenant: UCSF medical complex contracts

**Regulatory Navigation:**
- DPH data-sharing agreements (pre-negotiated)
- Planning Department zoning variances (expedited review)
- Prop H compliance (inclusionary housing credits)
- SFMTA parking permits for residents

**Employment Pipeline:**
- Salesforce, Stripe, Dropbox CSR partnerships
- Tech bootcamp integrations (Coding Dojo, Academy)
- Union apprenticeships (IBEW, Carpenter locals)
- Hospitality pipeline (Hilton, Marriott)

---

### Community Advisory Committee (CAC)

**Composition (10-15 members):**
- 3-4 formerly incarcerated residents
- 2-3 reentry service providers (Oakland + SF)
- 2 city officials (Oakland Housing + SF DPH)
- 2 community leaders (faith, neighborhood associations)
- 1 property developer/real estate expert
- 1 legal advisor (criminal justice, housing law)
- 1 funding partner representative

**Meeting Schedule:**
- Monthly (first Tuesday, 6 PM PT, alternating cities)
- Quarterly full-day retreats (strategic planning)
- Async decisions via Slack for urgent matters

**Decision Authority:**
- Resident screening policies & appeals
- Service provider partnerships
- Emergency funding allocation
- Community feedback integration

### Risk Distribution Strategy

| Risk | Oakland | SF | Mitigation |
|------|---------|----|----|
| Community opposition | Moderate | Low | CAC, community events, media |
| Zoning delays | Low | High | Pre-approved sites, DPH support |
| Property market volatility | Moderate | High | Dual-city hedge, off-market deals |
| Resident retention | Moderate | Moderate | Strong case management, incentives |
| Employment gaps | Moderate | Low | Tech industry pipeline (SF spillover) |

### Phase 1-2 Timeline (Weeks 1-12)

| Phase | Oakland | SF | Outcome |

| **1-2** | CAC formation, partner meetings | DPH negotiations, Prop H review | 10 CAC members, 5 MOUs signed |

### Success Metrics

- **Enrollment:** 50 residents (25 per city) by end Phase 2
- **Retention:** 95%+ housing stability at 6 months
- **Employment:** 60%+ job placement within 6 months
- **Community:** 0 significant opposition incidents, 8+ CAC meetings
- **Regulatory:** All zoning/compliance achieved on schedule
- **Cost:** $150K operations spend (under $3K per resident/year)
