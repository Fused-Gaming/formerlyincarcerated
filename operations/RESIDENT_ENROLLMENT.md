# Bitcoin Land Bond - Resident Enrollment Process

## Application & Screening (Weeks 8-10)

**Objective:** Attract 50-75 applicants, screen for program eligibility & housing readiness

**Week 8 - Application Launch:**
1. **Recruitment Campaign (Days 1-3)**
   - Partner organization outreach (Delancey, SVdP, CCRC, DPH reentry)
   - Social media & email campaigns (formerly incarcerated networks)
   - Community events (Oakland & SF neighborhood meetings)
   - Word-of-mouth through case managers (incentive: $100 referral bonus)
   - Target: 60-80 applications by end Week 9

2. **Online Application Portal (Days 3-5)**
   - Supabase database setup (applications, screening, case notes)
   - Simple form: Name, contact, background, housing history, needs
   - Auto-email confirmations & interview scheduling
   - Multilingual support (Spanish, Vietnamese - Bay Area demographics)

3. **Eligibility Verification (Days 5-7)**
   - Criminal record verification (Oakland DA, SF DA databases)
   - Must have justice system history (felony conviction requirement)
   - Income check (0-60% AMI acceptable)
   - Credit/rental history review (no automatic disqualifiers)

**Weeks 9-10 - Initial Screening:**
1. **In-Person Interviews (Ongoing, 30 min per applicant)**
   - Screen for: Housing readiness, stability, substance use (disclose honestly)
   - Verify identity, references (2 personal, 1 service provider)
   - Explain program requirements: Lease terms, case management participation
   - Collect emergency contact, employment history
   - Interview outcome: Approved, Conditional, Pending Additional Info

2. **Decision Criteria (Pass/Fail Rubric):**
   - Housing readiness: 40% (previous stable residence, references)
   - Program participation: 30% (willingness for case mgmt, drug testing if needed)
   - Financial stability: 20% (income verification, benefit eligibility)
   - Community fit: 10% (neighborhood alignment, no violent crime flag)
   - **Pass threshold: 70+ points** (most applicants should pass)

3. **Screening Outcomes (Target: 40-50 approved by end Week 10)**
   - Approved: Move to Dignifi verification phase
   - Conditional: Re-interview after specific condition met
   - Waitlist: High interest, lower priority (offer preference next cohort)
   - Declined: Clear feedback, resources for other housing programs

## Dignifi Verification (1-2 Weeks Per Resident)

**Objective:** Income verification, benefits eligibility, financial readiness confirmation

**Parallel to Case Manager Interviews (Weeks 9-11)**

**Process:**
1. **Income Documentation (Days 1-3)**
   - Bank statements (3 months) - verify deposits from benefits/employment
   - Benefit statements (SSDI, SSI, CalWORKs, TANF, unemployment)
   - Employment offer letters (if applicable)
   - Rental assistance eligibility pre-screening

2. **Dignifi Assessment (Days 3-7)**
   - Submit verified income to Dignifi platform
   - Dignifi generates financial capability report
   - Identify gaps in income (subsidize via program support)
   - Establish monthly support level ($300-800/month potential)

3. **Monthly Payment Setup (Days 7-10)**
   - Enroll in Dignifi wallet system
   - ACH or debit card link for benefit receipt
   - Case manager access to oversight features
   - Payment schedule aligned with lease (rent payment coordination)

## Case Manager Interview & Service Plan (Week 11)

**Objective:** Develop individualized support plan, establish ongoing relationship

**Activities:**
1. **Comprehensive Intake Interview (2 hours, Week 11 Days 1-7)**
   - Housing goals & barriers
   - Employment history & aspirations
   - Substance use, mental health, trauma (brief screening)
   - Family contact & reunification needs
   - Service preferences (counseling, job training, medical)

2. **Service Plan Development (Case Manager + Resident)**
   - Document goals: Employment (6 mo), Family contact (3 mo), Health insurance (1 mo)
   - Identify service partners: Mental health clinic, vocational rehab, food bank
   - Establish case management frequency (weekly check-ins, monthly deeper reviews)
   - Create accountability timeline (quarterly outcome reviews)

3. **Plan Documentation (Supabase case notes)**
   - Resident profile with all history
   - Service contacts & referral status
   - Payment arrangement & budget
   - Emergency contact protocols
   - Signed service agreement (witnessed by case manager)

---

## Lease Signing & Move-In Coordination (Week 12)

**Objective:** Finalize housing, execute occupancy, coordinate support transition

**Week 12 Activities:**
1. **Final Documentation (Days 1-4)**
   - Property inspection with resident
   - Lease walk-through (rights, responsibilities, house rules)
   - Move-in date confirmed (Week 12 Days 5-7 target)
   - Security deposit waiver (or program covers if needed)
   - Insurance requirements explained

2. **Lease Execution (Day 3-4)**
   - Sign lease (property manager + resident + witness)
   - Provide copy to resident, case manager, property manager
   - Record in Supabase (lease status: signed)
   - Verify renter's insurance availability

3. **Move-In Coordination (Days 5-7)**
   - Schedule utility activation (electricity, water, internet)
   - Coordinate furniture delivery (if applicable)
   - Welcome package (house rules, emergency numbers, services list)
   - First case manager home visit (introduce support system, baseline assessment)
   - Celebrate housing achievement (community welcome event)

## Key Success Factors

1. **Recruit early** - Partner org outreach starts Week 1, not Week 8
2. **Simple process** - Minimize bureaucracy (no credit score minimums, etc.)
3. **Clear communication** - Multilingual materials, written explanations of all decisions
4. **Genuine partnerships** - Case managers embedded in screening process
5. **Celebrate wins** - Public recognition of resident achievements builds community support
